# Northwind Cloud Support Plans

Northwind Cloud offers three levels of technical support.

## Plans

Community support is included with every account and is provided through the public forum and documentation. Standard support is included with the Business plan and covers email tickets during business hours. Premium support is available as an add-on for Enterprise customers and includes 24/7 phone support and a named technical account manager.

## Response times

Response times depend on ticket severity. For Premium support, critical (severity 1) tickets receive a first response within 15 minutes, at any time of day. Standard support responds to severity 1 tickets within 4 business hours and to all other tickets within one business day. Community forum posts have no guaranteed response time.

## Opening a ticket

Open tickets from the Support page in the console. Include the project ID, the affected resource IDs, the region and the time the problem started. Tickets about billing go to the billing team and are answered within two business days.

## Escalations

If a ticket is not progressing, you can request an escalation from the ticket page. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a support engineering lead within one business day.
