# Northwind Cloud Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## Eligibility

Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. After the first 14 days, monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Annual plans can be refunded on a prorated basis within the first 30 days. The refund covers the unused months of the subscription, minus any discounts that were applied at checkout.

## What is not refundable

Usage-based charges such as data egress, extra build minutes and premium support hours are never refundable, because they reflect resources that were already consumed. Domain registrations purchased through Northwind Cloud are also excluded.

## How to request a refund

Open the Billing page in the console and choose "Request refund", or email billing@northwind.example with your account ID and invoice number. The billing team replies within two business days.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## Service credits versus refunds

If an outage breaches our SLA, you receive service credits rather than a cash refund. Service credits are applied automatically to the next invoice and cannot be exchanged for money.
